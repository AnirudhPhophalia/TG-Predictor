Understanding your prediction

From a score to a perspective

Submit a total out of 100 for a subject. You can also enter optional exam, quiz, assignment and lab marks. These components are for reference; your total drives the prediction.

Before 100 submissions

Predictions use the subject's configured historical cutoffs. Default boundaries are A: 85, A-: 75, B: 65, B-: 55, C: 45 and D: 35. Defaults are estimates, not verified university records.

At 100 submissions and beyond

Your percentile is the number of students strictly below your score divided by all submissions, multiplied by 100. Tied scores receive the same percentile.

Your marks remain private

Only your signed-in account can see your individual submissions. Administrators manage subjects, polls and unlock requests, and see aggregate statistics. Admin statistics are hidden until at least five students respond.

Made a mistake?

Open your submission and request an unlock with a reason. An administrator can approve or reject it without seeing your score. After approval, update your marks; the submission locks again.

How reliable is the prediction?

The participation indicator (Low, Medium or High) rises as more classmates submit. It measures participation, not a statistical probability of correctness. Community data can be incomplete or unrepresentative.

Google login and admission year

Use your verified Thapar Google account. Addresses containing be_24 or be_25 are recognised as admission years 2024 and 2025. Other institute addresses can sign in; an undetected year stays blank. Your current semester comes from the admission year and today's date: odd semesters run July to December, even ones January to June. Overview and Explore subjects open on that semester; choose All semesters to see everything.
